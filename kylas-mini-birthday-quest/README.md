# Kyla’s Mini Birthday Quest

Open `index.html` in a browser. For iPhone Safari, upload the entire folder to any static host.

The supplied photos are loaded directly from `assets/kyla-amabella-1.jpg.jpg`, `assets/kyla-amabella-2.jpg.jpg`, `assets/trio-1.jpg.jpg`, and `assets/trio-2.jpg.jpg`. They appear in the photo Memory Mirror and gift reveal.

Controls: Stage 1 supports WASD/arrows and touch buttons; Stage 2 supports Space/Enter and its large tap button. The cinematic birthday letter rolls automatically, and includes pause, 2× speed, and skip-to-credits controls. All editable names, photos, full letter text, and credits remain in `CONFIG` at the top of `game.js`.
